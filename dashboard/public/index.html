ANVIL

Let AI agents spend. Know every purchase matched what a person approved.

Anvil sits between your agents and company money. It checks each request against the policy your finance team signed, asks a person when it matters, and keeps a record nobody can quietly edit.

  • Employees plan a trip with real dates, one way or round trip, and see exactly why each booking was approved, held or blocked.
  • Finance managers approve or reject whole trips that go beyond policy, with dates, cost centre and the reason in front of them.
  • Operators run the agents: pause one, read the audit trail and hand an auditor the evidence. They cannot release money.
